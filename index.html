L/Certified is Lexus’s certified pre-owned program. Each vehicle is a select low-mileage pre-owned Lexus that passes a comprehensive 161-point inspection by Lexus-trained technicians and is backed by Lexus Care. Lexus-trained technicians inspect the engine and powertrain, exterior and finish, interior, electronics and multimedia, safety systems and undercarriage, and complete a road test. Only vehicles that meet Lexus standards earn L/Certified status.

The L/Certified Limited Warranty provides at least two years of unlimited-mileage coverage with a $0 deductible. Program terms & disclaimers

L/Certified by Lexus vehicles are select pre-owned Lexus models, generally six model years or newer, that pass a comprehensive 161-point inspection performed by Lexus-trained technicians; L/Certified eligibility is based on a vehicle’s age and mileage at the time of certification.

Each L/Certified vehicle includes Lexus Care: the L/Certified Limited Warranty provides a minimum of two years/unlimited mileage with a $0 deductible, valid after the expiration of the four-year/50,000-mile new-vehicle Basic Warranty or two years from the L/Certified purchase or lease date, whichever occurs later; combined with the new-vehicle Basic Warranty, this can provide up to six years of coverage.

Lexus Care also includes complimentary maintenance covering the next four manufacturer-recommended services for two years or 20,000 miles, whichever comes first; 24/7 roadside assistance during the warranty period; trip-interruption assistance (lodging reimbursement up to $200 per night, plus meals and a rental vehicle); a complimentary loaner during covered warranty repairs; and trial subscriptions to SiriusXM and Lexus Connected Services. Covered repairs are made with no deductible.

The L/Certified Limited Warranty does not cover routine or scheduled maintenance, normal wear items (including brake pads, wiper blades and tires), or damage resulting from accident, misuse, alteration or lack of required maintenance; it is non-transferable and applies to the original L/Certified purchaser, and any remaining new-vehicle Basic Warranty transfers normally. The L/Certified Limited Warranty is governed by the terms of the L/Certified Limited Warranty Supplement provided with the vehicle.

Estimated $2,000–$2,500 added value reflects the combined benefits compared with a comparable non-certified used vehicle and varies by vehicle. Special financing on L/Certified vehicles is available through Lexus Financial Services with approved credit; not all buyers will qualify.
